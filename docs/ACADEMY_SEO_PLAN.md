# Artisan Barber Academy SEO Plan

Deferred while work is focused on the main Artisan Barber website.

## P0: accuracy, trust, and crawl quality

- Reconcile the conflicting Blueprint membership prices and connect or remove inactive enrollment controls.
- Verify or remove unsupported founder, licensing, teaching, experience, and student-count claims on `/about`.
- Publish substantive privacy and terms content for analytics, accounts, and enrollment.
- Confirm whether gifts are purchasable and verify their prices and fulfillment terms.
- Apply `noindex` and remove unfinished placeholder routes from the sitemap until they contain substantive content, including `/support`, `/newsroom`, and `/social-impact`.
- Confirm which courses are actually available. Exclude unreleased Refinement and Mastery pages from search until launch-ready.

## P1: commercial pages

- Give the homepage page-specific metadata and position the Academy around professional, client-service, and business fundamentals unless a broader training claim can be verified.
- Rewrite `/courses` around published programs and use a single descriptive H1.
- Clarify that Foundation is not licensing preparation or a replacement for supervised practical training unless accreditation is verified.
- Add real curriculum details, duration, lesson count, access period, instructor information, price, enrollment path, and completion terms to Foundation.
- Add `Course` structured data to real course pages and `ItemList` structured data to `/courses`.

## P1: journal

- Expand the two mock articles into substantive, expert-reviewed resources or remove them from indexing.
- Add author biographies, modified dates, original images, contextual course links, and editorial review information.
- Add the journal to primary navigation once it contains substantive content.
- Initial topics: barber consultations, client retention, pricing services, beginner tools, portfolio building, and online courses versus barber school.

## Technical backlog

- Add page-specific metadata and canonicals to public marketing routes.
- Add breadcrumbs and `BreadcrumbList` to course and article detail pages.
- Add a branded default social image and route-specific course/article social images.
- Add `dateModified` and image fields to article structured data.
- Remove dead social links.
- Add `FAQPage` only where the questions and answers are visible and substantive.

## Measurement inputs needed

- Search Console query and page exports for the previous three months.
- Search Console indexing, sitemap, Core Web Vitals, manual-action, and security reports.
